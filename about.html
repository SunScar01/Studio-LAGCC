About LAGCC STUDIO

Where student work gets seen.

A showcase built by LaGuardia students, for LaGuardia students. Every major, every project, one place.

Great work shouldn’t stay hidden in a classroom folder.

What STUDIO is for

  1. 01

    Show

    Give your project its own page: images, tools, your team, and the story behind the work.

  2. 02

    Discover

    Browse by major or category and find what other students across campus are making.

  3. 03

    Connect

    Save favorites, leave kind and specific feedback, and share one link with employers and transfer schools.
